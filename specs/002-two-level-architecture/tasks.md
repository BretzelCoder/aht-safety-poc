## Notes
- **Order follows the spec's order of value**: Part 1 (T-04 to T-12), then the visible parts (T-14 to T-18), the profile model coming early (T-05) because the link carries its delay.
- **Deliberate breakages** are made in the working copy, run, then reverted at once with `git checkout -- index.html`; none is ever committed.
- **A failing scenario or invariant** (existing or new) stops `/implement` and goes back to the spec, after re-measuring at the model's own step, threshold and over enough seeds (CLAUDE.md, "Re-measure before declaring a gap").
- **Test runtime** (plan, owner's point 5): measured at T-08 and T-12, and on GitHub at T-23. If the 5-minute budget is threatened, the long loops are spread across workers first; lowering a seed count needs a spec amendment.
- **Push** at T-23 only after asking; pull request and merge only on the owner's explicit request.
